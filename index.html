DCF Builder

Two-stage discounted cash flow: revenue growth and operating margin each move in a straight line from a Year-1 value to a Year-N value, cash flow is reinvestment-adjusted NOPAT, and the terminal value uses Gordon growth off the final forecast year. Unlevered free cash flow only — no explicit working-capital or D&A/capex detail, no probability weighting. The full scenario is encoded in the URL, so any link you copy reproduces this exact model.

Comps Builder

Enter 3–8 peers with trading multiples. Abacus computes the median and quartile range of each multiple, converts it to an implied value per share for your target, and blends that against an intrinsic estimate. EV/EBITDA implied values net out the target's own net debt to reach equity value; P/E and P/B multiply straight through to a price.

Bond Math

Fixed-coupon bond valuation off a single flat discount rate applied to every cash flow. No credit-spread curve, no OAS, no embedded options (call/put), no default risk. Accrued interest uses whatever elapsed-period fraction you enter — compute it Actual/Actual or 30/360, your choice, and it flows straight through.

Compounding & SIP

The lump sum compounds annually at your entered rate; the SIP assumes a fixed contribution invested at the start of each month (annuity-due). "Real" mode discounts the nominal rate by inflation via the Fisher equation and compounds on that basis — a simplification that implicitly assumes contributions rise with prices too, not a rigorous real-terms cash-flow model.
